## What the sources say

The Community Support Worker Instructor Lesson Plan Binder in the shared Community Support Curriculum folder is a **draft for instructor and program review**, not an accreditation or certification claim. It proposes eight months / 32 weeks, with weekly outcomes, a five-day sequence, preparation, practice evidence, safeguards and observation notes. It requires client dignity, consent, privacy, role boundaries and supervisor escalation; high-risk or restricted tasks require separate training, authorization and supervision. Placement is contingent on host approval and supervision. Preserve these qualifications wherever this source is represented.

The folder also contains individual weekly plans, student materials, an instructor marking guide and practical checklists. The separate Yonas Community Support folder contains the 25 CSW workbook pages and 25 CSWE picture checks used in the workbook library. The sets are treated as visual vocabulary teaching material, not as a 25-week curriculum or an accreditation-backed sequence.

## Coherent system contract

- One teacher entry point should lead to three Community Support teaching sessions, as confirmed by the owner. Their content must be grounded in the source documents, not inferred from titles.
- Teacher preparation should connect objectives, materials, modelling, guided practice, evidence and next steps. Point to the corresponding existing Student Practice stations instead of creating a competing student path.
- Student practice activity is not an instructor observation, certification or permission to perform a restricted task. Keep those distinct in interface and storage.
- Keep source references, document versions, draft status, access requirements and coverage gaps visible. Do not store real client details in browser demo storage or publicly shipped files.
- Design for small, usable teaching sessions while retaining the full source curriculum and its duration as separate information. Do not claim three sessions replace an eight-month program.

## Research principles

Colorado Department of Education's [curriculum implementation guidance](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/standardsandinstruction/support/curriculum-adoption-and-implementation) emphasizes coherent materials, continuing professional learning and collaboration. [NIET's implementation guidance](https://www.niet.org/assets/1da4c1fbd6/high-quality-curriculum-implementation.pdf) connects objectives, instructional decisions, student work and feedback. Apply these as design principles; neither source validates this specific curriculum.
