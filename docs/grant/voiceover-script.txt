Purpose Academy grant explainer — voiceover script
Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (length-scale 1.06)
Runtime target: ~60–90 seconds

1. Purpose Academy is a Calgary trades school learning app — for newcomers and first-time trades learners who want workplace English with adult dignity.

2. Three pathways open the door: Construction, Logistics, and Community Support Foundations.

3. On desktop, you open the toolbox — Practice, Student, Instructor, or Admin — then step onto a calm night stage.

4. Practice keeps one station in focus. Finite path. No rush. No exam theatre.

5. The vocabulary loop is simple. See the picture. Hear it in your home language. Hear the English workplace word. Then type it.

6. Or clear an Eye Spy board — tap the picture, name it in English, until the board is empty.

7. Soft learner chrome. Done for now when you need to stop. Built for adults who may fear school — never childish gamification.

8. Progress points toward employment readiness and a Skills Passport: learned, practised, competent. Practice alone is not a trade ticket or a job guarantee. Competent still needs a real instructor.

9. Purpose Academy. Built in Calgary — one station at a time.
